About Wabi Sabi Coffee Shop

Welcome to Wabi Sabi, where we embrace the beauty of imperfection and find joy in the simple things.

Our Mission

Wabi Sabi’s mission is to create a safe space for people to take a break and be in the moment in an ever-changing world.

Our Vision

Wabi sabi is a traditional Japanese saying that translates to “one finding quiet and deep meaning in imperfection”. Our coffee and matcha are of high quality.

We are inspired by building community through centered spaces.

Ready to taste it? Explore our matcha and coffee menu, see the photo gallery, or visit us in Pretoria East.
